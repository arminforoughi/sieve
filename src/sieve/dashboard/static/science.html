    Methods & caveats

    Force field
    SieveUFF: UFF Lennard-Jones on every framework atom; TraPPE CO₂ (3-site, rigid). Lorentz–Berthelot mixing, 12.8 Å cutoff, potential shifted, no tail corrections. No framework partial charges (RASPA ChargeMethod None).
    Simulation
    RASPA2 grand-canonical Monte Carlo, 298 K, rigid framework, absolute loading in mol/kg (= mmol/g). Supercells sized for the cutoff. Uncertainty is RASPA's block-average σ.
    Tiers
    T0 descriptors only (CoRE MOF/Zeo++: PLD, LCD, void fraction, density, metals); no uptake.
    T1 GCMC 100 init + 500 cycles at 1 bar, nominal ±18%: not converged, used only to rank.
    T2 GCMC 400 init + 2000 cycles at 1 bar, nominal ±8.6%: the only tier a winner may be named on.
    This page
    Isotherm: 150 init + 500 cycles at 0.05–5 bar. Pockets: RASPA's 0.5 Å energy grid for the CO₂ oxygen probe; "strong" = below −10 kJ/mol. Run outside every screening budget and cached in data/science/.
    Capture
    Flue gas: 15:85 CO₂:N₂, 1 bar total, 298 K. Binary GCMC (swap + identity-change moves, 200 init + 500 cycles) with TraPPE N₂ (2 LJ sites, 1.10 Å) added to the same force field; no charges on any molecule, as for CO₂.
    Selectivity S = (xCO₂/xN₂)/(yCO₂/yN₂), x = adsorbed loadings in the mixture, y = 0.15/0.85; error from both loadings in quadrature. S₀ = KH(CO₂)/KH(N₂) is the infinite-dilution limit.
    WC VSA = NCO₂(0.15 bar, 298 K) − NCO₂(0.01 bar, 298 K), pure CO₂. WC mixture = CO₂ loading from the flue-gas mixture − NCO₂(0.01 bar, 298 K): the more realistic number. WC TSA = NCO₂(0.15 bar, 298 K) − NCO₂(0.15 bar, 393 K). All in mol/kg.
    Qst = −(⟨Ugh⟩ − ⟨Uh⟩ − RT) at infinite dilution from Widom insertion (2000 cycles, 298 K), kJ/mol, positive = exothermic; the GCMC fluctuation estimate at 0.01 bar is shown alongside. KH from the same Widom run, mol/(kg·bar). Qst is a whole-molecule energy; the "deepest well" is one CO₂-oxygen site, so Qst is typically 1.5–2.5× its magnitude.
    Caveats
    Without charges, CO₂'s quadrupole never meets the framework's field, so electrostatically binding sites (open Mg²⁺/Cu²⁺, amines) are missed: Mg-MOF-74 comes out far below experiment. For the same reason CO₂/N₂ selectivity and Qst are underestimated for open-metal-site and polar MOFs. No framework flexibility and no water: dry flue gas only, no water competition (which in reality hits open-metal-site MOFs hardest). Treat all values as a ranking signal for this model, not a lab prediction.
